03·Principles

Four commitments. Non-negotiable.

The rules of the road for a public value network.
I

Decentralised.

No single point of control, ownership, or failure.

The network is governed by everyone who runs it.

II

Permissionless.

Anyone can join, subject to rule of land.

No gatekeeper. No vendor to sign up with.

III

Open standards.

The specification is the network.

Not owned by any one company, country, or consortium.

IV

User-centric.

Your identity, your assets, your data. Under your control.

Fabric never becomes the middleman.

04·Agents are first-class

Built agent-native. From the ground up.

Every primitive works for an autonomous agent the way it works for a human.

Most networks were built for humans, with agents bolted on. Fabric is built the other way. An agent on Fabric has its own verifiable identity, its own wallet, its own reputation that travels with it. It can discover, negotiate, contract, and settle — with humans, businesses, or other agents — at machine speed, under a policy envelope set by its principal.

⬡ AGENT IDENTITY
Verifiable, portable, revocable.
⬡ DELEGATED AUTHORITY

⬡ PER-TASK SETTLEMENT
Pay per inference. Pay per call. Pay per outcome.
